Patterns in nursing EHR activity

Studying when EHR interactions occur within a shift, instead of relying only on total activity.

Status
Ongoing research
Data type
Nursing EHR audit logs
Time scale
5-minute bins; 30-minute windows
Focus
Within-shift temporal representations

The problem

A total count of EHR interactions does not describe how activity is distributed across a shift. Short bursts, quiet intervals, and recurring sequences can be lost when the data are aggregated.

Research approach

My work explores a shapelet-informed representation of nursing activity. A 30-minute window comprises six consecutive 5-minute intervals. Moving the window through a shift supports investigation of local patterns in their broader temporal context.

My focus

  • Prepare shift-level time series from binned EHR interactions.
  • Represent short activity windows and examine recurring patterns.
  • Explore clustering and visual summaries for within-shift activity.

What this demonstrates

Applying time-series methods to clinical workflow data while keeping the meaning and limitations of the source data in view.

Research in progress. EHR interactions are a record of system use, not a complete measure of nursing workload or quality of care. This brief does not claim validated clinical outcomes.
